LEGEND:
================================================================================
X       = Seat/Bed position
|       = Aisle or separation
Driver  = Driver position (front of bus)
FRONT   = Front of the bus
BACK    = Back of the bus

================================================================================
NOTES:
================================================================================
- Limousine 9 & 16: Single floor buses with reclining seats
- Cabin Riêng: Private cabin compartments (1-1 layout, two floors)
- Giường Nằm 32: Double-decker sleeper bus with 16 beds per floor
- Giường Nằm 4X: Premium double-decker sleeper with 20 beds per floor
